Titanic Passenger Survival Analysis

I used Titanic Passenger Survival Prediction Dataset from Kaggle

Chart 1: Vertical Survival Rate by Class & Gender

I decided on this being my basic chart because I think it gives the best overall understanding the coparison between the survival rate of a man vs a woman in different passenger classes.

In this grouped chart it shows the mean survival percentages of genders in different passenger classes

Chart 2: Horizontal Survival Rate by Class & Gender

I made this variation to help reduce visual clutter as well as make the chart easier to read and understand, as well as changing the colors to a more unique color palette.

In this horizontal chart it shows the same information as the first chart, the mean survival rate of geners in different passenger classes

Chart 3: Horizontal Stacked Number of Passengers by Class & Gender

I was not completely sure if we were expected to change the specific data points but for this chart I changed the survivabilty rate to the total number of passengers.

I chose this type of chart to help visualize the differences in specific passenger classes of men versus women.

This layered chart shows the total amount of passengers on the Titanic in each passenger class and is categorized by gender.

Chart 4: Normalized Stacked Survival Rate by Class & Gender

In this last chart, I chose it because of it's ability to show the ratios of each gender in different passenger classes.

This stacked chart shows the ratio of women versus men surviving passengers from their respective passenger classes.
